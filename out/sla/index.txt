<h2 id="5-service-availability-and-maintenance">5. Service availability and maintenance</h2><div class="docs-table-scroll" role="region" tabindex="0" aria-label="Scrollable data table"><table><thead><tr><th>Measure</th><th>Service commitment</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Monthly production Availability target</td><td>99.5% per calendar month</td></tr><tr><td>Scheduled maintenance window</td><td>Sundays, 00:00–04:00 WAT</td></tr><tr><td>Advance notice of Scheduled Maintenance</td><td>At least 72 hours where practicable</td></tr><tr><td>Emergency maintenance</td><td>As much advance notice as reasonably practicable, with post-event communication</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p><strong>Measurement.</strong> Monthly Availability is calculated as <code>(eligible service minutes − verified Downtime minutes) ÷ eligible service minutes × 100</code>, rounded to two decimal places. The monitored endpoints are <code>https://app.teecomsintegratedsolutions.ng/</code> and <code>https://api.teecomsintegratedsolutions.ng/digitalinvoice-server/api/plans</code>. External monitoring is scheduled <strong>once per minute</strong>. For the purpose of this SLA, a Downtime incident is recognised after <strong>two consecutive failed monitoring checks</strong> (at least two consecutive one-minute intervals), confirmed against application and incident records. Teecoms will investigate credible Customer evidence of service interruption. Only intervals when a covered core service is unavailable count as Downtime.</p>
<p>Maintenance notices will be delivered through the product, email or Customer notice channels, identifying expected impact and duration where reasonably possible. Emergency security fixes may occur outside the maintenance window. The parties may document variations in a signed agreement.</p>

The parties will cooperate on investigation, remediation and notices to affected data subjects.</p>
<p>Teecoms&#39; Information Security Management System is <strong>certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022</strong> (certificate <strong>SCK/05/TIS/26/23/9804</strong>, issued by <strong>SCK Certifications Pvt. Ltd.</strong>, expiry <strong>19 November 2029</strong>, conditional on successful surveillance audits), with a scope including software and application development, e-invoicing, cloud and hosting services. Independent DigitalInvoice vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (<strong>VAPT</strong>) is <strong>in progress</strong>; the report will be made available to Customers on request when complete. <strong>No NRS MBS production accreditation is claimed.</strong> See the <a href="/developers/security/">security and certification overview</a> for further information.</p>
