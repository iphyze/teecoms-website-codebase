6:T15d0,<h2 id="scope-and-authenticity">Scope and authenticity</h2><p>These documents demonstrate the layout and data presentation of DigitalInvoice&#39;s <strong>own PDF document system</strong>, including business-to-business (B2B) invoices, business-to-consumer (B2C) simplified invoices, credit notes and payment receipts. The downloadable files are built using the document model and PDF templates from the DigitalInvoice application, with fixed <strong>fictional test data</strong>.</p>
<aside class="docs-callout" data-tone="info" aria-label="Fictional document illustrations"><strong class="docs-callout-title">Fictional document illustrations</strong><div><p>All records, business names and amounts used here are invented for demonstration. The verification-field examples contain explicitly mock IRN, CSID and QR details. They illustrate design and field positioning, <strong>not an official NRS validation or clearance result</strong>.</p>
</div></aside><h2 id="two-presentation-layouts-per-document">Two presentation layouts per document</h2><div class="docs-table-scroll" role="region" tabindex="0" aria-label="Scrollable data table"><table><thead><tr><th>Layout</th><th>What the PDF displays</th><th>Purpose</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><strong>Standard document layout</strong></td><td>The regular DigitalInvoice PDF design with fictional seller, buyer, line, tax and amount details</td><td>Show the appearance of each document type</td></tr><tr><td><strong>Illustrative verification-field layout</strong></td><td>The same PDF design with mock <strong>IRN</strong>, <strong>CSID</strong> and <strong>QR</strong> fields</td><td>Show the intended visual placement of verification details; values are illustrative only</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>The demonstration QR contains a mock-only message instead of an official verification reference. The payment receipt illustration refers to a fictional linked invoice. <strong>Phase 5 does not add a signing service, a cleared invoice PDF, or a verified production IRN/CSID/QR</strong>, so these examples and their binary PDFs/PNGs have not been altered to imply such outcomes.</p>
<h2 id="documents-illustrated">Documents illustrated</h2><div class="docs-table-scroll" role="region" tabindex="0" aria-label="Scrollable data table"><table><thead><tr><th>Document type</th><th>Content</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>B2B tax invoice</td><td>Invoice lines, discount, VAT calculation, totals, business buyer details</td></tr><tr><td>B2C simplified invoice</td><td>Consumer invoice with one VAT-rated line</td></tr><tr><td>Credit note</td><td>Adjustment reason, original invoice reference, negative credit total</td></tr><tr><td>Payment receipt</td><td>Receipt amount, allocation and illustrative invoice reference</td></tr></tbody></table></div><h2 id="visual-sample-gallery">Visual sample gallery</h2><p>Eight document cards appear immediately below this section: one for each document type and each of the two layouts. Each example includes a fictional PDF and matching PNG preview, available for download from this gallery. The samples demonstrate presentation and document fields, not NRS acceptance.</p>
<aside class="docs-callout" data-tone="info" aria-label="File integrity and publication"><strong class="docs-callout-title">File integrity and publication</strong><div><p>The eight PDF/PNG pairs are published as illustrative samples. The static site verifies that matching PDF and PNG files are available together, and the examples remain separate from production NRS evidence.</p>
</div></aside><h2 id="phase-5-integration-status-and-sample-boundaries">Phase 5 integration status and sample boundaries</h2><p>DigitalInvoice&#39;s <strong>backend</strong> now has permission-controlled, sandbox-gated reference-code synchronisation, local official-code preflight and an approved-invoice NRS sandbox <code>/validate</code> request path. These features are <strong>not document generation features</strong> and have not been independently run against a live authorised NRS sandbox in this review. An accepted sandbox validation response would not amount to signing, clearance, production evidence or a new sample certificate.</p>
<p>The downloadable PDFs and PNG previews remain <strong>eight fictional visual illustrations</strong>, not certificates or proof of tax-authority validation. See the <a href="/developers/api/">API documentation</a> for sandbox and production integration boundaries.</p>
<h2 id="generation-and-verification">Generation and verification</h2><p>The generator belongs in the DigitalInvoice <strong>application root</strong> containing the PDF templates (<code>scripts/generate-nrs-samples.mjs</code>) and runs without API credentials, database access or an NRS network connection. The Teecoms site&#39;s <code>docs/BUILD-GUIDE.md</code> documents the exact generation command, prerequisites, paths and publication checks.</p>
<p>All examples use fictional data and mock-only verification fields. They are provided so integrators and reviewers can inspect document layouts without handling production customer records or authentic NRS-issued identifiers.</p>
<h2 id="related-documentation">Related documentation</h2><p>See the <a href="/developers/api/">API documentation</a> and <a href="/developers/data-structure/">invoice data structure</a> for the local integration model, data fields and explicit licence restrictions. The <a href="/sla/">Service Level Agreement</a> is approved, effective <strong>27 November 2026</strong>; fictional samples are not official NRS validation evidence.</p>
