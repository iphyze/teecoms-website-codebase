6:T2ea4,<h2 id="programme-overview">Programme overview</h2><p>DigitalInvoice is Teecoms Integrated Solutions Limited&#39;s electronic invoicing platform. This timeline summarises the platform development milestones, security assurance programme and planned steps towards authorised NRS MBS integration. The programme is sponsored by Teecoms management, with technical delivery led by the CTO.</p>
<p><strong>Status as at 25 November 2026:</strong> The invoicing application, local UBL-style invoice preparation, ERP integration interface, authentication safeguards and developer reference materials are implemented. Teecoms Integrated Solutions Limited <strong>is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022</strong> (initial registration <strong>20 November 2026</strong>; <a href="/developers/security/">certificate details</a>). The DigitalInvoice independent VAPT is <strong>in progress</strong>. NRS production submission remains disabled pending onboarding, successful interoperability testing and production licensing.</p>
<aside class="docs-callout" data-tone="info" aria-label="How to interpret the dates"><strong class="docs-callout-title">How to interpret the dates</strong><div><p><strong>Completed</strong> identifies milestones documented in the project&#39;s October/November 2026 records; for some earlier development work, a precise original start date was not separately recorded. <strong>Target</strong> identifies a planned milestone, not a regulatory approval or guaranteed launch date. <strong>NRS-dependent</strong> activities start only after the relevant NRS authorisation and credentials are issued. The indicative windows in the chart move if those external dates change.</p>

<p><strong>Parallel assurance path:</strong> independent VAPT fieldwork → report → remediation → retest. Independent assurance evidence and production security readiness must be completed before go-live. Pilot UAT, support staffing, training and any certificate enrolment run alongside these workstreams and also require successful sign-off.</p>
<p>Production NRS clearance, licensed signing responsibilities and genuine NRS-issued identifiers are <strong>not presented as currently operational</strong>. The target Q1 2027 go-live remains conditional on NRS decisions, secure integration testing, VAPT completion and management approval.</p>
